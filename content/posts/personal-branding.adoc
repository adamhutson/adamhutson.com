Personal branding is a new concept that I've just recently discovered.  
The idea is that your professional and personal lives are interconnected.  
Meaning that what you do after work is just as important as what you do during work.  
This new blog is the first step of many to brand myself.  
I am really excited about this new self-packaging approach.  
Hopefully, it will give a better perspective of what goes on in my whole life, what drives me, what I'm thinking about, and where I'm going.  
The whole picture can be viewed through this blog, and my personal Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn accounts.  
Happy reading!
